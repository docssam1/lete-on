02 · ONE SENTENCE TO POLISH

ORIGINALAt first, everything seemed very fine until Audrey suddenly touched the glowing gem and fell asleep in the middle of the street.

SUGGESTIONAt first, everything seemed fine. But when Audrey touched the glowing gem, she suddenly fell asleep in the middle of the street.

왜 이렇게 추천할까요? “very fine”에서 불필요한 “very”를 빼면 더 자연스럽고, 긴 문장을 둘로 나누면 사건의 원인과 결과가 또렷하게 보여요.

Why this change? Removing “very” sounds more natural, and splitting the long sentence makes the cause and result easier to follow.

✓ The original stays saved. The suggestion appears separately.
