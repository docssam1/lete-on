# K–12 competition source audit — 2026-08-28

## Purpose and decision rule

This is a source-and-access audit, not a licence opinion.  Links below are
first-party organizer or standards-owner sources.  A public organizer page,
including one labelled “free,” is not treated as permission to copy, translate,
embed, or redistribute its questions, artwork, answer keys, or solutions.

| Status | Meaning in this audit |
| --- | --- |
| **Verified** | The stated fact is visible on an official page linked below. |
| **Access-gated** | An organizer records the resource, but sign-in, membership, enrollment, payment, or a country partner may be required. |
| **Absent / unverified** | No qualifying organizer source was located in the review scope; this is not a claim that no copy exists anywhere. |

## Verified current competition facts

### SASMO (SIMCC)

- The current [SASMO organizer page](https://sasmo.simcc.org/) says the competition is open to **K2 and Grades 1–12**.  Grade 1–12 papers are level-differentiated, **90 minutes**, **25 questions**, and **two sections**; K2 is **60 minutes**, **15 questions**, and **two sections**.  The listed content strands combine school mathematics with non-routine problem solving.
- The current [registration page](https://sasmo.simcc.org/register/) states that the platform may be online or paper, depends in part on the country partner, and that registration/account requirements apply.  Its 2026 listing says LMS access includes **2023–2025** contest papers and answer keys.

### MAA AMC 8, AMC 10, and AMC 12

- The [MAA AMC overview](https://maa.org/student-programs/amc/) describes **AMC 8** as 25 questions in 40 minutes for Grade 8 and below (under 15.5); it covers middle-school mathematics with later questions that may touch beginning algebra.
- The same official source describes **AMC 10** as 25 questions in 75 minutes for Grade 10 and below (under 17.5): elementary algebra, basic geometry, area/volume, elementary number theory, and elementary probability; trigonometry, advanced algebra, and advanced geometry are excluded.
- It describes **AMC 12** as 25 questions in 75 minutes for Grade 12 and below (under 19.5), covering the full high-school curriculum including trigonometry, advanced algebra, and advanced geometry; calculus is excluded.
- The [current MAA policy page](https://maa.org/student-programs/amc/maa-american-mathematics-competitions-policies/) says its rules govern U.S./Canada administration and may change.  The overview also says students/parents in the U.S. and Canada do not register directly with MAA; an eligible competition manager administers the contest.  Product claims and delivery must therefore remain preparation-only, use the live policy as the authority, and never represent this project as an AMC site or registration channel.

### Math Kangaroo USA

- The official [test FAQ](https://mathkangaroo.org/mks/faqs/about-the-test/) specifies a **75-minute multiple-choice** examination for **Grades 1–12**: 24 questions in Grades 1–4 and 30 in Grades 5–12.  Kindergarten participants may take the Grade 1 paper.
- The official [previous-year PDF index](https://mathkangaroo.org/mks/practice/pdf-exams/) organizes original past tests by grade pairs (1–2 through 11–12) and says the PDFs may be viewed or printed.  That availability is evidence of an official access path, not a blanket rehosting or translation licence.

### Common Core high-school mapping caution

The [Common Core mathematics landing page](https://corestandards.org/mathematics-standards/) links the official standards.  In the [official high-school standards PDF](https://corestandards.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Math_Standards1.pdf), the categories are Number and Quantity, Algebra, Functions, Modeling, Geometry, and Statistics and Probability.  They are a coherent view of high-school mathematics, and work with functions crosses conventional course boundaries.  Therefore these categories must **not** be presented as grade levels, fixed course titles, or a complete competition syllabus.

## Private archive: internal-only custody record

Current internal summary: **18 PDF assets; 715 pages; 13,016,541 bytes; SHA checks completed.**  This is an internal integrity/custody record, not publication metadata and not evidence of a licence.

At audit time, the acquisition pass downloaded every direct organizer-hosted PDF asset exposed by the verified 2019/2020 listing lanes.  Account- or enrollment-gated courses were catalogued but were not bypassed or represented as downloaded.

Independent file review found three organizer-label conflicts and locked them rather than inferring coverage: the 2020 Grade 4 and Grade 5 URLs contain 2019/2020 solutions-only files, while the organizer's Grade 10/JC listing names Grades 10–12 but the downloaded file itself identifies only Grade 10.  Grades 11–12 remain unverified for that asset.

- Never public-rehost, proxy, cache, embed, export, or translate these PDFs, including diagrams, question text, answer keys, and solutions.
- Keep hashes and access records private; do not put filenames, extracts, page images, OCR, or derived question text into this repository or public build.
- Any future use requires a fresh source/right review and an explicit approved delivery path.  Neither an internal checksum nor public visibility of a separate organizer page changes that requirement.
